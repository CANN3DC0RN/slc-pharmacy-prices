Save prices

For Beehive, The Forest and Bloc, whose menus can't be read automatically. About a minute per store, and doing it weekly keeps prices fresh.

1

Add the bookmark (one time only)

Drag this button onto your browser's bookmarks bar. If you don't see the bar, press ⌘ Shift B (Mac) or Ctrl Shift B (Windows).

⬇ Save prices

Works in Chrome, Edge, Safari and Firefox on a computer.

2

Open a pharmacy's menu

Wait until you can see products, then click Save prices on your bookmarks bar. A file like beehive-slc.csv downloads in a few seconds.

  • Beehive Farmacy (Salt Lake City)
  • The Forest (Murray)
  • Bloc Pharmacy (South Jordan)
3

Upload the files

Open the upload page, drag in the downloaded .csv files, and click Commit changes. The site updates in about 3 minutes.

Open the upload page
How freshness works: each store shows "prices checked X days ago" on the site. Prices more than 14 days old are hidden automatically, so the site never shows old prices as current.
