How we collect these prices

Prices come from each pharmacy's own online menu, at the medical (cardholder) price. Some menus are checked automatically several times a day; others are saved by hand about once a week, and the "Prices checked" column above shows when. Prices change often, so confirm with the pharmacy before you go.
